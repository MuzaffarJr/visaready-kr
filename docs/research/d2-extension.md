## Summary

- **Legal basis.** Extension of stay is governed by 출입국관리법 제25조, 시행령 제31조 and 시행규칙 제32조 (S2). The documents to attach are listed by visa status in 시행규칙 [별표 5의2] (개정 2024. 12. 24.) (S1).
- **D-2 documents in the law (별표 5의2).** For a regular course at a junior college or higher: enrollment certificate (재학증명서) and proof of finances for tuition and living costs (재정(학비, 체재비) 입증 관련서류). For specific research: proof of research activity (S1). Every applicant also submits the application form, passport, residence card and fee (S6).
- **MOJ manual adds two items:** proof that the student is studying normally (학업을 정상적으로 수행하고 있음을 입증하는 서류) and proof of residence (체류지 입증서류) (S6). gov.kr also lists 체류지입증서류 (S2).
- **Fee: KRW 60,000** for extension of stay. Basis: 시행규칙 제72조, version effective 2026-01-23 (S3). gov.kr shows the same amount (S2). An extension does not appear to require a new residence card. If a card is issued or reissued, the fee is KRW 35,000 (S3).
- **Timing.** You must apply before your stay expires (법 제25조; S4). The MOJ Visa Navigator (Ver 2022.12) says you can apply from 4 months before expiry. Office applications are accepted up to the expiry date itself, and e-applications up to the day before (S5).
- **Channels.** Online through HiKorea (전자민원), a visit to the immigration office with a reservation, or by post (post is for island residents only) (S2, S4). Processing time is 15 days, or 60 days if investigation is needed (S2).
- **Financial-proof waivers and amounts come from university pages and an older MOJ guideline, not current law.** Several 2026 university notices say bank proof is waived when GPA is at least 2.0. One adds attendance of at least 70% (S9). Amounts differ between sources (see Conflicts).

## Timing and channel

- **Deadline.** Apply before your period of stay expires (출입국관리법 제25조; S4).
- **Window.** From 4 months before expiry, up to the expiry date for office applications or the day before expiry for e-applications (S5, MOJ Visa Navigator Ver 2022.12). Not re-confirmed on a 2026 official page, so treat this as **partly confirmed**.
- **Channels.** HiKorea online e-application (전자민원) with document upload and online payment (S2, S8, S11). Visit to the immigration office that covers your address, with a HiKorea reservation (S4, S11). Post, for island residents only (S2). University notices also mention an agency route (HireVisa) (S9, S10).
- **Processing time.** 15 days in general, 60 days if investigation is needed (S2).
- **Length of stay granted.** Up to 2 years per grant for D-2 (S6, S7). Students with a D-level GPA may get only up to 6 months (2022 guideline, S7; current rule UNCONFIRMED).
- **Penalty.** A fine of at least KRW 100,000 for overstaying without an extension. This comes from a university notice only (S11), so the official amount is UNCONFIRMED.

## Conflicts and open questions

1. **Fee amounts at universities do not match the law.**
   - The law (S3) and gov.kr (S2) say KRW 60,000.
   - Seoultech (S9) says "50,000 KRW (individual application)" and 65,000 through HireVisa.
   - An Inha University PDF title shows "수수료 70,000원" (search result only; fetch failed).
   - Older PDFs from SNU (2021) and JNU (2022) show 65,000 in search snippets (not fetched).
   - These may include agency or card fees, or may be errors. **Treat 60,000 as the official figure.**
2. **Financial-proof amounts and waiver rules.**
   - Seoultech (S9): KRW 10,000,000 in the capital region and 8,000,000 elsewhere, for extensions of 1 year or more.
   - Chosun (S10): at least KRW 8,000,000. For an extra semester: 1,150,000 × months × ½.
   - 2022 MOJ guideline (S7): for **initial** finances, USD 20,000 in the capital region and USD 18,000 elsewhere for degree courses, and USD 9,000 for language trainees. Proof can be waived on extension if GPA ≥ C (2.0).
   - The current MOJ guideline was not retrieved. **Current official amounts are UNCONFIRMED.**
3. **Supervisor's recommendation for thesis students.** gov.kr (S2) lists it. Two extraction passes over 별표 5의2 (S1) disagreed: the first mentioned it as a substitute document, the second did not. The original file needs a manual check.
4. **Transcript and tuition receipt.** Neither appears in 별표 5의2 for an extension. Both come from the MOJ manual's "proof of normal academic progress" item and from university practice.
5. **Residence proof.** gov.kr and the MOJ manual require it. The 별표 5의2 extraction did not show it. It may be required under a general rule for all statuses (law article UNCONFIRMED).
6. **"Apply from 4 months before expiry."** The only source is the 2022 Visa Navigator. It was not re-confirmed on a 2026 HiKorea page.
7. **HiKorea pages.** Fetches of the HiKorea info pages returned mismatched or navigation-only content, so they could not serve as primary sources for D-2 extension details.
8. **Not established:** a TB test for extension, part-time work documents, and whether students at accredited universities (인증대학) are officially waived from financial proof (S10 claims so).
9. **D-2 sub-types (D-2-1 to D-2-8)** and their differences on extension were not researched.
